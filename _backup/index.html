FAQ

Questions & Answers

How is this different from other chatbot services?

Most chatbots just collect information and email it to you. Appointly AI connects to your live calendar, checks real-time availability, and places the actual appointment on your schedule — no human intervention needed. We also prevent double bookings by building in buffer times between appointments.

Which calendars and CRMs do you integrate with?

Google Calendar for scheduling, plus HubSpot, Airtable, and Google Sheets for CRM. Custom integrations are available on the Enterprise plan.

Can the chatbot handle rescheduling and cancellations?

Yes. Customers can book, reschedule, or cancel entirely through the chatbot. Calendar updates in real-time and confirmation emails are sent automatically.

How does it prevent double bookings?

The chatbot queries your Google Calendar for all existing events before presenting available times. It calculates open slots based on your business hours, meeting duration, and configurable buffer times. Only genuinely open slots are offered.

Can the chatbot answer questions about my business?

We load your business information, FAQs, services, and policies into a knowledge base. The chatbot uses AI-powered retrieval (RAG) to deliver accurate answers to any question.

How long does setup take?

Most chatbots are live within a few business days. We handle configuration, calendar integration, CRM setup, and knowledge base loading. You provide the details, we do the rest.
